Advent of Watchmen - 2025 - Issue 3

Advent of Watchmen - 2025 - Issue #3

This is part three of my 2025 Advent of Watchmen series, which will run from 12/12/2025-12/24/2025.

What surprised you most about this issue?

Rorschach saying, “Are you sure?” in response to Bernard’s observation that the world didn’t end yesterday as he’d said it would. This is the first time I’ve reread Watchmen since seeing Oppenheimer and, of course, this brings to mind the scene between Oppenheimer and Einsten when Oppenheimer says he believes we did destroy the world.

Which single panel stood out most?

It has to be the six-panel, iconic image of Dr. Manhattan sitting on Mars and holding the photo of himself and Janey. It’s an absurd image on its face and yet highly emotionally engaging – you can feel every bit of his consternation. It’s hard to imagine just what he’s feeling, though. Is it actual sadness, loneliness, regret and/or longing? Or, is he sad that he can no longer feel those emotions or connect with people?

The art itself is gorgeous and the contrast in colors between the black of space, the red of Mars and the blue of Dr. Manhattan is something to behold. I believe the digital version I’m reading has been recolored and I would very much like to know how this version of the image contrasts with the original one.

What did this issue go on to influence? Alternatively, what was this issue influenced by?

The way Tales of the Black Freighter is used is an established plot device known as a “frame narrative” or “intercalation”. Personally, I find this instance of it annoying and would have preferred to just have the Watchmen prime universe handle any exposition it’s providing. I could see it being a fun exercise for the creators, though, and its presence does give us some nice moments between Bernard and Bernie.

Also, I’d be remiss if I didn’t note that the previously mentioned scene on Mars has spawned an entire ecosystem of memes and cute references in other forms of media.
