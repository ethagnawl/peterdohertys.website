Advent of Watchmen - 2025 - Issue 2

Advent of Watchmen - 2025 - Issue #2

This is part two of my 2025 Advent of Watchmen series, which will run from 12/12/2025-12/24/2025.

What surprised you most about this issue?

How difficult to read The Comedian’s backstory is. I knew the rape and murder scenes were coming but I’d forgotten they were in the same issue. It’s by design, of course, but they are extremely effective at making you hate him on a visceral level.

Which single panel stood out most?

The panel in the Crimebusters meeting where Ozymandias is reviewing the burned map (the result of more awful behavior by The Comedian) while Wally Weaver implores somebody to save the world. Is this the genesis of Ozymandias’ plan? He’d probably have thought of it anyways, being the world’s smartest man and all, but it’s a nice bit of color added to his arc if nothing else.

What did this issue go on to influence? Alternatively, what was this issue influenced by?

You do have to wonder if The Comedian’s attack of Silk Spectre was at all influenced by the infamous, violent outburst in Avengers #213 in which Hank Pym (Ant-Man) hits his wife, Janet (The Wasp). It’s also very possible that Alan Moore was unaware of that incident and the reaction to it and this is just a coincidence. I would imagine Moore was probably pretty well checked out on what else was going on in “mainstream” comics by this point.
