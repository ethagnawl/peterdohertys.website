# [Advent of Watchmen - 2025 - Issue #4](https://amzn.to/4qbku8i)

This is part four of my 2025 _Advent of Watchmen_ series, which will run from 12/12/2025-12/24/2025.

### What surprised you most about this issue?
How literal some of the watch and watchmaker references are.

### Which single panel stood out most?
The panel in which Dr. Manhattan concludes his assessment of The Comedian. It describes a great number of people "working mostly for the government" these days.

### What did this issue go on to influence? Alternatively, what was this issue influenced by?
The six panel image of Jon Osterman being _taken to pieces_ would seem to be an obvious influence for the current [Geiger](https://amzn.to/4pRCLrF) series By Geoff Johns and Gary Frank.
